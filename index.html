Support Escalation AI

The project that taught me the most

Confidence 72%
Illustrative routing · drag the slider

Most support systems face the same problem: answer too much and they become unreliable. Escalate too much and they stop being useful. I wanted to explore a middle ground.

Support Escalation AI uses retrieval-augmented generation and agent-based workflows to retrieve relevant information, answer common issues, and escalate complex cases when confidence is low.

What I built
  • Tier-1 and Tier-2 support agents
  • Semantic document retrieval using FAISS
  • Confidence-based escalation
  • Authentication and conversation history
  • Interactive Streamlit interface
Built with: Python, LangGraph, Gemini API, FAISS, Sentence Transformers, Streamlit, Supabase

IndicPulse

Understanding how people actually write

Illustrative tagging · pick a sentence

People rarely stick to one language when they communicate online. A single sentence might contain English, Hindi, Punjabi, slang, abbreviations, and spelling variations all at once.

IndicPulse is my attempt to better understand code-mixed Indian languages through NLP and transformer-based approaches.

Exploring
  • Intent Classification
  • Emotion Detection
  • Language Identification
  • Code-Mixed Text Processing
  • Multilingual NLP
